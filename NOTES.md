Amethyst is warm and confident: deep plum ink on cool lavender white, with violet as the one accent.

Buttons
- One primary button per screen or card, for the main action. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
- Buttons are pills. Danger is only for actions that delete or cannot be undone, and its label names the thing ("Delete invoice").
- Labels are verbs, sentence case, at most three words.
- Sizes: md by default, sm inside tables and dense toolbars, lg only for a page's single hero action.

Colour
- Violet (primary) means "go" and "selected". Rose means danger or error. Amber means warning. Never use them as decoration.
- Text is plum on lavender or white; muted text only for secondary info.
- Body text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark.

Type
- Fraunces (serif) for headings only. DM Sans for everything else. JetBrains Mono for numbers in tables and codes.
- Sentence case everywhere, including headings and tabs.

Layout
- 4px spacing grid; card padding 24px; 16px between related items, 32px between sections.
- Cards have a 20px radius; inputs 8px; buttons and tags are pills.
- Use a card only to set one object apart. A list of rows is a list, not a stack of cards.

Don't
- No gradients, no emoji.
- Don't put two primary buttons side by side.
